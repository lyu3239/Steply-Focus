Privacy Policy for Steply Focus

This Privacy Policy explains how Steply Focus handles information when you use the app.

Home Terms of Use Support

Last updated: March 28, 2026

Overview

Steply Focus is designed to help users break tasks into smaller steps and use simple, low-pressure focus tools. In the current app build, task data, task steps, focus sessions, and app preferences are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly use a platform feature that processes information outside the device, such as Apple-managed speech recognition or App Store billing.

Information We Process

Depending on how you use the app, Steply Focus may process:

  • Tasks and step breakdowns you create inside the app
  • Focus session settings and history stored on your device
  • Voice input captured only while you actively use the voice task feature
  • Support emails you send to us

Microphone and Speech Recognition

If you choose to create tasks by voice, Steply Focus requests microphone and speech recognition permissions. Audio is used only to support your voice input action while you are actively using that feature. Speech recognition may be provided by Apple system services, and Apple may process audio or transcripts according to its own policies.

Subscriptions and Payments

Steply Focus offers auto-renewable subscriptions. Payments are processed by Apple through your Apple ID account. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Data Sharing

Steply Focus does not sell your personal information. The current app build does not use third-party advertising networks.

Data Retention

Data you create inside the app remains on your device unless you delete it. If you contact support by email, we may keep that correspondence for customer support purposes.

Your Choices

You can stop using voice input at any time and can manage permissions in your device settings. You can also delete app content directly inside the app or by removing the app from your device.
